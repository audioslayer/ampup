More color. More rhythm. Better control. 🎛️

Amp Up 1.3.7 expands room lighting with new effects, reworked music visualizers, and hands-on tuning controls—plus fixes for mute feedback and device reliability.

**🌈 Give your room a new look**

- Explore 22 new room effects, including **Firefly Garden, Sparkler, Black Hole, Lava Lamp, Hyperdrive, Laser Grid, Sunrise, and Shimmer**.
- Choose from soft palette-driven motion, cinematic patterns, and bright RGB effects.
- New effect artwork and expanded N3 Space templates put room lighting controls at your fingertips.

**🎵 Turn your music into light**

Six reworked VU modes bring different parts of your music to life:

- 🎤 **Voice Glow** — vocal-driven flashes across the room.
- 🪞 **Mirror EQ** — a mirrored spectrum with peak sparkle.
- 🌧️ **Beat Rain** — beat-triggered rain with layered color trails.
- 💥 **Bass Bloom** — bass hits that spread outward from the center.
- 💎 **Prism EQ** — fifteen frequency bands painting the lights independently.
- 🌊 **Gravimeter** — a gravity-driven volume column with a falling peak dot.

**🎛️ Tune the response**

- New rotary controls for beat frequency, bandwidth, noise gate, attack, and release.
- Mode-specific controls for rain density, trails, beat flash, gravity, and more.
- Simplified Room controls and music-reactive layers over room effects.
- Improved paired-device ordering for rain and gravity effects.

**🔇 Mute feedback that follows your mix**

- **Pos+Mute** keeps the progressive LED fill and dim mute indicator.
- **Blend+Mute** colors the whole dial through the volume gradient and returns to the zero-volume color when muted.
- Both follow the knob’s linked VoiceMeeter strip/bus, app group, master, microphone, or selected audio device.
- AmpUp-triggered VoiceMeeter mute changes notify lighting immediately; manual changes are polled about once per second.
- Fixed app-group mute detection for names such as **Apple Music**, including Windows audio-session display names.
- Clearing an app group now clears stale mute lighting.

**⚡ Smoother response, fewer hiccups**

- Improved bass resolution while retaining roughly **23 audio updates per second at 48 kHz**.
- Corrected FFT scaling to preserve audio-reactive sensitivity and removed redundant spectrum calculations.
- Fixed a spectrum frequency-boundary error.
- Fixed a Corsair discovery recursion crash and reduced repeated discovery attempts during iCUE recovery.
- Fixed Govee H61A0 rope-light power-off behavior.
